YOUR NAME — MUSIC WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: page structure and content
- styles.css: styling and responsive layout
- script.js: menu, sharing, music player, reveal effects, back-to-top and basic form checks
- audio/: place your MP3 files here
- images/: place your images here
- downloads/: place your downloadable ZIP/PDF files here

TO CUSTOMIZE
1. Replace YOUR NAME, CITY, COUNTRY, artist IDs, social links and email addresses.
2. Add real audio files with these exact names in audio/:
   midnight-drive.mp3, neon-rain.mp3, golden-hour.mp3, static-dreams.mp3
3. Add your image files and update the artwork/background CSS as desired.
4. Replace placeholder video IDs and ticket links.
5. Netlify forms work only when the site is deployed/configured on Netlify; they do not send email from a plain local HTML file.

LOCAL TESTING
From this folder, run:
  python -m http.server 8000
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in your browser.

This package does not include music, artwork, or download assets because those were not supplied.
